### Research and analytics (code private, happy to walk through it)

- **CineCosine, movie recommendations for OTT.** A hybrid of semantic similarity and a Bayesian quality score raised the average rating of top-5 picks from 5.91 to 7.24 / 10 at 0.4 ms per query, across 4,800 TMDB movies.
- **Landslide risk mapping, re-testing a published claim.** Over 30 seeds, showed that a reported AUC of 0.96 – 0.99 mostly measured distance to roads; road-matched sampling gives an honest 0.65 – 0.76.
- **Customer churn prediction.** ANOVA / Chi-square feature selection and threshold tuning: Recall 72.7 %, ROC-AUC 83.4 %.
